The Problem We Solve

Modern Healthcare Is Overwhelming

"Design and develop an AI-powered Personal Health Copilot that helps individuals understand, organize, and manage their healthcare journey." — AItrix Labs Challenge
🔬

Opaque Lab Results

Patients receive blood panels full of abbreviations and numbers with no plain-English explanation of what they mean.

⚠️

Symptom Uncertainty

People don't know when to call 911, visit Urgent Care, or rest — leading to dangerous delays or costly ER over-utilization.

💊

Medication Complexity

Managing multiple medications with timing, dosage, interactions, and adherence is cognitively demanding without assistance.

📋

Wasted Doctor Time

Without a structured briefing, patients forget key data during short 15-minute clinical visits, leaving critical questions unasked.

How It Works

Dual-Mode Clinical AI Architecture

Feature 1 of 5

Command Center
Dashboard

Your daily health command center — a real-time clinical snapshot of your complete wellness status.

  • 📊 Live wellness score from vitals, adherence & lab data
  • 🩺 Quick symptom triage bar — urgency level in seconds
  • 💓 BP, glucose & heart rate metric cards with flags
  • 💊 Today's medication checklist with one-tap logging
  • 🔬 Latest abnormal lab findings at a glance
Feature 2 of 5

Diagnostic Lab
Explainer

Transforms cryptic blood panels into clear, actionable clinical insights. No medical degree required.

  • 🔬 One-click load: CMP, Lipid Panel, HbA1c presets
  • 📊 Visual 3-zone gauge bars (Low / Normal / Elevated)
  • 💡 Plain-English "what this means" for every biomarker
  • 🎯 Actionable lifestyle guidance per out-of-range value
  • 💬 "Discuss with Copilot" AI deep-dive on any marker
Feature 3 of 5

Clinical Copilot
& Symptom Triage

Empathetic AI that risk-stratifies any symptom into one of four clinical urgency tiers — instantly.

  • 🚨 EMERGENCY (911) — cardiac, stroke, respiratory distress
  • 🟠 URGENT CARE — same-day evaluation warranted
  • 🟡 ROUTINE CLINIC — schedule appointment this week
  • 🟢 SELF-CARE — monitor and continue wellness
  • 🗣️ Text-to-Speech accessible audio playback
Feature 4 of 5

Medication &
Vitals Tracker

Clinical-grade medication adherence management with interactive biometric trend visualization.

  • 💊 Morning/Afternoon/Bedtime schedule with dose logging
  • 📈 Daily adherence % meter and streak tracking
  • ⚠️ Drug-drug interaction scanner (Lisinopril + Potassium etc.)
  • 📉 SVG trend charts — blood pressure & glucose over 14 days
Feature 5 of 5

Doctor Visit Prep
& Health Summary

Auto-generates a structured, printable clinical briefing so patients maximize every minute with their physician.

  • 📋 Executive briefing: diagnoses, allergies, physician info
  • 💓 14-day vitals averages in one consolidated view
  • 🔬 All out-of-range biomarkers highlighted for discussion
  • ❓ AI-generated, prioritized physician questions
  • 🖨️ One-click Print / Save as PDF with medical styling
